About Kingsize Global

A Lagos logistics company built on careful handling and honest communication.

Who we are

Kingsize Global Logistics Enterprises Ltd is based in Ajah, Lagos. For more than ten years we have delivered goods safely within Lagos State and to other states in Nigeria.

We specialise in new house and property Relocation for individuals and organisations, office equipment moves, Warehouse Relocation, delivery of building materials, and Hiab lifting of home and company generators. For heavy loads we run high-tonnage trucks, lowbeds and open-body trucks.

Our team of eight logistics attendants, haulage workers and consultants plans each job with you and keeps you informed until delivery.

How we work

  • TransparencyClear prices and honest updates, with no surprises on the day.
  • ExpertiseYears of experience with homes, offices, warehouses and heavy equipment.
  • Client firstWe plan around your goals, schedule and budget.
