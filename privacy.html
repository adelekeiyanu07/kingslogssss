Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Kingsize Global Logistics Enterprises Ltd ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you use our quote form, call us or message us on WhatsApp, you may share your name, phone number, pick-up and delivery locations and details of what you want moved. We use this only to prepare quotes, plan and carry out your job, and contact you about it.

The quote form

The quote form on this site does not store your details. It opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply once you send it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with crew members and partners who need it to complete your job, or when the law requires.

Fonts and third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address when the page loads.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email logisticshaulage@gmail.com or kingsizelogistics18@gmail.com, or call 0812 318 6010.
